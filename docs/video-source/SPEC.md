# Greenhouse — One team, many ways to work

GOAL: Invite a team to self-host Greenhouse and connect their agents.
OUTPUT: 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, 60 seconds (1800 frames).
LOOK: #FAFBF8 ground, #203B31 ink, #235D4D accent, #B6CA68 seed. Nunito and bundled Noto Sans SC. Titles 74px/800; support 32px. Real app images occupy 65–85% of frame, with focused crops. 96px margins, caption safe band 880–980px.
MOTION: Titles enter over 0.5s with power4.out and hold. Screen panes ease over 0.7s with power2.inOut; fade-through adapted from the official registry between scenes. One focal move per scene.
SOUND: Chinese narration, rendered locally with macOS Tingting for this preview. No paid provider or account required. No music bed: none supplied, and no signed-in catalog/local music model. Keep voice clear. Timed English and Chinese WebVTT accompany the MP4.
