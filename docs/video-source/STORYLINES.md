# Storylines

Recommended: A, because one real question carried through the product is what a stranger remembers.

## A. One question, followed (built)

Device: the result first. Form: object-to-object chain through one window.
The film opens on a real chart answer, pulls back to the chat that produced it, then follows the answer to its sources (the Tables rows, the cited policy), out to the member's own agent, the activity log and the budget, onto the iPhone, and into a plugin. Each shot is entered through something on the previous one.
First two seconds: the bar chart of average score per channel fills the frame; "Which feedback is negative?" blurs in above it.
Beats: hook (chart) → pull back (chat) → source rows → citation → policy → your agent → scopes → activity log → budget → iPhone → plugin → end card.
Look: the product's own ground #F6F8F7, ink #1C1C1E, forest #235D4D, seed #B6CA68 rationed to one mark per shot; Nunito 800 titles; real 4.0 captures only.
Sound: silent. It autoplays muted on the website, so large titles and the product's own text carry it.

## B. Monday at nine

Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous take across web and phone.
A support lead opens Greenhouse on the train (iPhone), asks about last week's complaints, and finishes at the desk (web) where an automation already wrote the standup.

## C. The governance layer, said in type

Device: type carries the argument. Form: cut on the beat.
Giant kinetic words (permissions, audit, budgets, plugins) cut against half-second flashes of the matching real screen; needs a music bed to work.
