# Film review — 2026-10-26

The film follows one team workflow: connect an agent, find grounded answers, work with tables
and projects, reuse templates and automations, continue on native devices, then inspect access
and cost. All product imagery is captured from the running application with fictional data.
There are no invented interactions, customer endorsements or generated product screens.

Reviewed the first and last frame, every scene's entry and midpoint, all seven scene cuts,
and the internal screenshot crossfades. Readable titles were checked at phone scale. Fixed
the title line break, the iPad crop and the empty midpoint during screenshot transitions.
The final HyperFrames check reports zero lint, runtime, layout, contrast and craft findings.

Screenshot stages deliberately hold while their narration explains the feature (`data-hold`).
UI text is supporting evidence; the large headline and narration carry the message on a phone.
The weakest shot is the service pricing overview, where detailed table labels require pausing
at full size. A longer tutorial could show that workflow in a dedicated close-up.

The Chinese voice is local macOS Tingting synthesis. There is no music bed. Audio is measured
for duration, loudness and clipping after rendering; it has not been assessed by listening.
The delivered file measures 60.000 seconds, 1920 × 1080, 30 fps, H.264/AAC, integrated
loudness −14.0 LUFS and true peak −1.5 dBFS. English and Chinese subtitle tracks are embedded
in the MP4 and also supplied as WebVTT for the website. This is a product overview, not an
exhaustive training video for every settings screen.

The installed CLI's optional contact-sheet generator could not load its Sharp native library.
Individual PNG snapshots succeeded; contact sheets were assembled locally from those original
frames for review. The final video renderer uses its normal Chromium/FFmpeg pipeline.
