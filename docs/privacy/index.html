Greenhouse

Privacy Policy

Applies to the Greenhouse app for iPhone and iPad and to the hosted web client. Last updated: 2026-11-01

Greenhouse is open-source software that an organization runs on its own server, called a station. The app is a client for a station: you choose the station's address and sign in with an account that station gave you. The app's authors operate no station and no backend for it, and receive none of your data.

Where your data goes

  • Everything you do in the app — messages, files, documents, records, settings — is sent only to the station you signed in to and is stored and processed there under that organization's policies. The station may forward your requests to the AI model and other services it has configured.
  • When the app hits an error, it sends a report to the same station: the error name, a redacted message and stack, the screen's route, the app version and the platform. It contains no message content. Station administrators can read these reports.
  • If you allow notifications, the station sends them through the Expo push service, which relays them to Apple. A notification carries its title, a short text and a link inside the app.
  • The app checks Expo's update service for app updates. That request carries the app's version and platform, not your account or content.
  • There are no analytics, advertising or tracking SDKs, and nothing is sold or shared for advertising.

What stays on your device

  • The station addresses you added and the sign-in tokens for each (in the iOS Keychain). Your password is not stored.
  • Preferences such as language and appearance, unsent drafts, and copies of recent items for offline reading.
  • For the widgets, a small snapshot of recent conversations and tasks in the app's shared container.

Signing out of a station removes its tokens and cached copies. Deleting the app removes everything it stored.

Photos, files and camera

The app reads a photo or file only when you pick one to attach or upload, and sends it to your station. It does not scan your library.

Your account and deletion

Your account belongs to the station's organization. Ask its administrators to export or delete your data; they can remove accounts and content on the station.
